iCabbi CC Processing SOP

Step 1 of 5
1

Start of Shift: Signing In

At the beginning of each shift, you must sign into both the iCabbi Driver App and the iCabbi Companion App.

iCabbi Driver App

  • Tap the Driver App icon.
  • Enter your Driver # and PIN # (both are the same as your Driver #).
Important: No leading zeros. For example, if your Driver # is 969, your PIN is also 969.

Companion App

  • Open the Companion App.
  • Enter your Driver # (same as the Driver App).
  • Once logged in, the Companion App is ready for processing.
2

During Shift: Initiating Payment

A

Complete the Trip

Turn the meter off after completing the trip. In the Driver App, select "CASH" when prompted for payment (there will only be CASH and ACCOUNT options). This clears the trip from the Driver App.

B

Process via Companion App

In the Driver App, click the floating "Pay by Card" icon to automatically open the Companion App. Completed trips will be listed in chronological order (the trip you just completed will be at the top). Select the trip, then click "Pay by Card."

3

Running the Transaction

The credit card machine is now engaged. Confirm with the customer if they would like to add a tip:

Yes, Tip

Press the Green button on the terminal.

  • Choose a default percentage (15%, 20%, 25%).
  • Confirm the tip amount with the customer.

No Tip

Press the Red X button on the terminal to completely bypass the tip screen.

Once the tip (or no tip) is confirmed, the machine will prompt for payment. The customer should tap their card on the reader to complete the transaction.
4

Confirming Successful Payment

Check the iCabbi Companion App. The completed trip will now be highlighted in green and will read "Paid by Card", indicating the payment has been successfully processed.

That's it—all done!

5

End of Shift: Signing Out

At the end of your shift, verify that all trips have been processed and payments completed.

You must sign out of both the iCabbi Driver App and the Companion App.
