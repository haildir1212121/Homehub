01Why Oregon Taxi

Always safe,
always trustworthy.

Founded in Eugene in 2004, we've grown into one of the leading private transportation companies in the Northwest — and the largest taxi fleet in Eugene and Springfield. The promise is simple: safe, clean and dependable, every ride.

Vetted, professional drivers

Every driver passes state and federal background checks plus a sex-offender registry check, has no DUIs, and completes a two-day course covering classroom, on-the-road and sensitivity training.

Cleaned & inspected daily

Cabs are cleaned and inspected every day for lights, brake function, tire pressure and fluid levels, with regular scheduled maintenance across the entire fleet.

On time, by the numbers

A large, responsive dispatch center fields over 1,000 calls a day with an 11-minute average time to pickup — consistently ranking us among the top cab companies for on-time performance.

04Drive With Us

Be your own boss.
Keep the support.

Flexible hours, little or no experience required. As an independent contractor you set your own schedule and build your own clientele — backed by the highest-volume dispatch center in the Eugene–Springfield area.

Driver benefits

  • Flexible scheduling — many shifts, including nights and weekends
  • Discounted leases for new drivers
  • Largest taxicab fleet in Eugene–Springfield
  • Entire fleet equipped with GPS tracking

Free training

  • 12 hours of training, completely free
  • Company orientation on day one
  • Road certification on day two
  • Eligible to start as soon as training is complete

Basic requirements

  • Valid Oregon license & legal US resident
  • At least 23 years old, licensed 39+ months
  • Clean 5-year MVR, no DUI/DWI in the last 5 years
  • No more than 2 moving violations in 39 months
